How to play

  1. Set up. Put your phone or laptop at chest height, landscape works best. Stand 1.5–2 m back in good light.
  2. Grip. Hold both hands together like a bat, or hold a stick (a broom handle or rolled newspaper) with a bright coloured tip: tape, a sock or a ball.
  3. Calibrate once. It learns your stance, reach and swing speed.
  4. Time it. Start your swing as the ball comes past the halfway mark after it bounces.
  5. Aim. The ball goes the way the bat moves. Swing straight for a straight drive, across to the right for the right side, across to the left for the left side. Early timing pulls it to the leg side, late timing to the off side.
  6. Loft or ground. Swing upward through the ball to loft it in the air. Swing downward to keep it on the ground.
  7. Lofted shots can be caught. Don't miss a straight ball, or you'll be bowled.
