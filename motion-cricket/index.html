Swing for real. Hit for six.

Settings

If your shots are always "Early", slide right; always "Late", slide left.

How to play

  1. Set up. Laptop or phone at chest height, landscape. Stand 1.5–2.5 m back in good light.
  2. Your bat. Any stick works: a rolled newspaper, a broom handle, a toy bat. It is easiest to see if it's a different colour from the wall behind you.
  3. Calibrate once. The game learns your stick, your stance, your reach and how fast you swing.
  4. Timing. Make the fastest part of your swing meet the ball as it reaches you. Early goes to the leg side, late to the off side.
  5. Direction. The ball goes the way your bat moves: straight, left, right, or anywhere in between.
  6. Height. Swing up through the ball to hit it in the air, down to keep it along the ground. Lofted shots can be caught!
  7. Six meter. Every six shows how far it went. Beat your longest!
